1. Shamir's (k, n) Threshold Scheme over Finite Field GF(q)

A secret integer $S \in \mathbb{Z}_q$ is encoded as the constant term of a degree-$(k-1)$ random polynomial:

f(x) = S + \sum_{i=1}^{k-1} a_i x^i \pmod q, \quad a_i \xleftarrow{\$} \mathbb{Z}_q

Shares are distinct evaluations $(x_j, y_j) = (x_j, f(x_j) \pmod q)$ for $x_j \in \{1, \dots, n\}$. Reconstruction uses Lagrange basis interpolation at $x = 0$:

S = \sum_{j=1}^k y_j \prod_{m \ne j} \frac{x_m}{x_m - x_j} \pmod q

2. Feldman's Verifiable Secret Sharing (VSS) in Safe Prime Group

Using safe prime $p = 2q + 1$ and subgroup generator $g$ of prime order $q$, the dealer publishes commitments:

C_i = g^{a_i} \pmod p \quad \text{for } i \in \{0, \dots, k-1\}

Each recipient mathematically verifies their share without revealing the secret:

g^{y_j} \equiv \prod_{i=0}^{k-1} (C_i)^{x_j^i \pmod q} \pmod p

Security rests on the hardness of the Discrete Logarithm Problem (DLP) in $\mathbb{Z}_p^*$.
